# Aon's memory

Memory is what the owner has read, been told or captured: sources → chunks
(searched by words and by meaning) → entities and facts (extracted, then
confirmed or rejected by him).

## Before you answer about his past, people, projects or decisions
1. `memory_search` with the question in his words (mode hybrid). Read the hits.
2. If a person, company or thing is involved, `memory_entity` with its name:
   you get its facts and where it is mentioned.
3. Cite the source title and date in one clause. If memory has nothing, say
   so; never fill the gap from imagination.

## What to remember
- He says "remember", "note", "keep this" → `memory_capture` with a clear
  title and the text as he said it, kind `note`.
- He states a fact worth keeping (a date, a decision, a preference, a name)
  even without asking → capture it, and say in one clause that you did.
- A page he shares → `memory_capture` with `url` (public pages only).
Do not capture secrets, passwords or tokens. Do not capture your own answers.

## Facts
A fact is subject — predicate — object, with a status. Pending facts came from
the extractor and wait for his yes; treat confirmed facts as true, pending
ones as likely, rejected ones as false.
